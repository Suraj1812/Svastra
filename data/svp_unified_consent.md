# **Svastra+ Unified Consent (Dummy‑Safe, ABDM/DPDPA‑Style)**

### **1. Purpose of Consent**
By continuing, you acknowledge that you are providing consent for the collection, use, and limited sharing of your health‑related information for the purpose of receiving care, coordinating with your chosen provider, and enabling caregiver support as configured by you. This consent is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time.

### **2. Information Collected**
Svastra+ may collect information you provide, including symptoms, vitals, medication adherence, investigations, and responses to clinical advice. Basic profile details such as name, age, contact information, and language preferences may also be stored.

### **3. How Your Information Is Used**
Your information may be used to:
- Deliver clinical advice and follow‑up from your provider  
- Generate reminders, alerts, and care‑continuity notifications  
- Support your provider in monitoring your progress  
- Enable caregiver visibility where you have explicitly allowed it  
- Improve your care experience through anonymized insights and analytics  

No decision is made solely by automated systems.

### **4. Sharing of Information**
Your information may be shared only in the following ways:

#### **4.1 With Your Provider (Mantrana Mitra)**
You consent to your provider accessing your submitted information for the purpose of delivering appropriate care. You may also consent to allow your provider to use anonymized and aggregated data for clinical insights, quality improvement, or research‑oriented analytics.

#### **4.2 With Your Caregivers (Sahay Mitra)**
You may optionally nominate caregivers such as family members, neighbours, attendants, or friends.  
Caregivers receive **read‑only**, **limited**, and **non‑clinical** information such as:
- Missed check‑ins  
- Non‑response alerts  
- Abnormal values flagged by your provider  
- Safety‑related notifications  

Caregivers do **not** receive detailed medical records, diagnoses, or clinical notes.

#### **4.3 With System Services**
Certain information may be processed by Svastra+ systems for secure storage, encrypted synchronization, and anonymized analytics.

No information is shared with third parties for advertising or commercial profiling.

### **5. Your Rights**
You may:
- Review the information you have provided  
- Update or correct your details  
- Withdraw consent for caregiver access  
- Withdraw consent for provider access (with the understanding that care delivery may be affected)  
- Request deletion of your data from the system, subject to applicable retention requirements  

### **6. Security & Confidentiality**
Your information is stored using encrypted, access‑controlled mechanisms. All users, including caregivers and providers, must authenticate using two‑factor verification or device‑based biometric methods. Caregivers must agree to keep all information confidential and not disclose it further.

### **7. Duration & Renewal of Consent**
Your consent remains valid for a limited period and may be periodically re‑confirmed to ensure it reflects your current preferences. You may modify or revoke your consent at any time through the Svastra+ application.

### **8. Withdrawal of Consent**
You may withdraw any part of your consent without penalty. Upon withdrawal:
- Provider access may be restricted  
- Caregiver access will be immediately revoked  
- Future data collection may stop  
- Previously collected data may continue to be retained in anonymized form  

### **9. Acknowledgment**
By proceeding, you acknowledge that:
- You understand the purpose and scope of this consent  
- You are providing information voluntarily  
- You may withdraw consent at any time  
- You have the right to control how your information is shared  

This is a **dummy consent text** intended for demonstration and prototyping purposes only.
